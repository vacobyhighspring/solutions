How up-sell commission works

$0 – $5Mclient cumulative GM

Full monthly commission and the 20% year-end bonus.

$5M – $10Mclient cumulative GM

Monthly commission stops. The year-end bonus keeps paying.

Over $10Mclient cumulative GM

Fully capped. No commission or bonus.

How long are you paid?

Commission is paid monthly as long as GM flows, up to $5M cumulative GM per client. There is no time-based cap; the 18-month cap was removed.

Do new deals with the same client count?

Yes. Each new deal earns its own scorecard allocation and bonus.

Does recurring or renewal revenue count?

Yes. As long as GM flows, you earn commission (up to the $5M cap) and the bonus each year.

Do multiple up-sell deals stack?

Yes. Three deals means three scorecard allocations and three times the bonus.

Do cross-BU referrals count?

Yes. Referring a client to another business unit, for example Consulting to Talent, qualifies.

What about a new contact at the same client?

Yes. New contacts or expansions at an existing client count as new up-sells.

A single recurring deal pays you every year. Stack five deals and you earn five times as much. That is the power of up-selling.
