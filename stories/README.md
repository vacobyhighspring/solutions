# Case Studies & Client Success Stories

Live page: https://vacobyhighspring.github.io/solutions/stories/

## Sharing one story
- **Link:** open a story and tap **Copy link**. Links look like `.../stories/#case-29`.
- **Standalone file:** tap **Download HTML**. You get a single file showing only that story, which you can email or upload anywhere.

## What it is
One searchable library of every technology and digital story: our Northeast team's case studies and client success stories, plus all technology case studies and client success stories from vaco.com and highspring.com (94 total as of Nov 2026). Filter by Service (Consulting, Managed Services, Staff Augmentation, Executive Search, Direct Hire), Area of Expertise, Industry, Source and Location.

Case studies from the websites keep a link to the original page, and every story can be downloaded as its own HTML page.

## Adding a story
Send Claude the details (a PDF, a slide, a link or plain notes). It needs: title, client description (no client name unless it's already public), industry, services, challenge, solution, impact, and any numbers. Claude adds it to the `CASES` list in `index.html`, tests it and publishes it.

## Monthly refresh
A scheduled task checks the vaco.com and highspring.com story libraries each month. Any new technology story is added and published.

## Naming
- **Case Study**: published on vaco.com or highspring.com; links to the original.
- **Client Success Story**: our Northeast team's engagements. Client names withheld; not client references. A disclaimer appears in the footer of every page and download.
