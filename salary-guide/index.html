Methodology

How we built this guide

Every figure is what the market is paying now. Nothing is projected or estimated.

Sources

  • Vaco by Highspring internal data.
  • U.S. Dept. of Labor (BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025): New York-Newark-Jersey City metro, plus New Jersey and Connecticut statewide.
  • 1,430 job postings with posted salary ranges in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut, posted in the last six months (May 10 to November 10, 2026), from Google Jobs, company career sites and job boards.
  • Salary sites: Glassdoor, PayScale, Salary.com, ZipRecruiter, Built In, Comparably and Levels.fyi, New York figures, collected November 2026.
  • Bonus and long-term incentives: SEC proxy filings of public companies headquartered in the New York area, and published executive pay surveys.

How the numbers are set

  • Each source counts once. The typical salary is the middle of the sources’ typical figures; the range is the middle of their low and high figures (25th and 75th percentiles where published).
  • Job postings: the middle of posted low and high ends, and of the midpoints. Duplicates, hourly and contract roles, and postings older than six months are excluded.
  • Financial Services figures use postings from banks, insurers, investment firms and private equity, plus Vaco internal data. Accounting & Finance figures exclude those employers.
  • State, industry and experience views use only real postings and appear when there are at least 3 postings.
  • Base annual salary unless stated. Bonus and equity figures are shown separately, with their sources.
