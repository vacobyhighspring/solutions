iWhat's in our burden?

Burden is our cost of employing or contracting the worker, on top of their pay rate.

W2 · 25%We are the employer of record

  • Payroll taxesEmployer Social Security and Medicare (FICA, 7.65%), plus federal and state unemployment taxes (FUTA / SUTA)
  • Workers' compensationRequired insurance; the rate depends on the state and the job
  • Liability insuranceGeneral and professional liability coverage for consultants on assignment
  • Benefits & required leaveHealth benefits we offer under the ACA, state-mandated paid sick leave, and other benefits
  • Payroll & onboardingPayroll processing, timekeeping, background checks and drug screens

CTC · 5%The consultant's company is the employer

  • No payroll taxes or benefitsThe consultant's own company covers its taxes, insurance and benefits, which is why the burden is much lower
  • Compliance & vettingChecking the vendor's business registration, insurance certificates and contracts
  • Classification riskProtection against a corp-to-corp worker later being treated as our employee
  • Admin & payment termsVendor setup, invoice processing, and paying the vendor before the client pays us
Margin is after burdenGross margin = bill rate − (pay rate + burden). Burden dollars never count as margin.
Markup is before burdenMarkup is on pay rate only, so the same markup gives a lower margin on W2 than on CTC.
Quote with marginW2 and CTC candidates at the same pay rate need different bill rates to hit the same margin.
