Employment, handled

Everything Philippine law requires, managed for you.

Highspring runs employment under the Philippine Labor Code and related laws, so you get a compliant team without building a local entity.

Pay & premiums

Payroll & contributions

Payroll, tax withholding, SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG are handled locally by Highspring.

13th-month pay

Required by law: at least one-twelfth of annual basic pay, paid by December 24.

Night shift differential

At least 10% extra for every hour worked between 10 PM and 6 AM, which covers most U.S. daytime support. It stacks on top of overtime.

Overtime

At least 25% extra for hours beyond eight in a day, and 30% on rest days and holidays.

Holidays & rest days

Regular holidays are paid at 200% when worked, special non-working days and rest days at 130%. Highspring publishes the official calendar each year.

Minimum wage

Set by regional wage boards and updated periodically. Highspring keeps pay compliant with the current order.

Leave

Service incentive leave

5 paid days a year after one year of service, typically covered by company PTO.

Maternity leave

105 paid days, with 15 more for solo mothers. Up to 7 days can be shared with the father or another caregiver.

Paternity leave

7 paid days for qualifying married fathers.

Solo parent leave

7 paid working days a year for eligible solo parents.

Leave for victims of violence

Up to 10 paid days for women employees who need medical or legal help.

Special leave for women

Up to 2 months paid after surgery for a gynecological condition.

Hiring & probation

Employment contracts

Regular or fixed-term contracts, with remote, hybrid, or onsite arrangements.

Standard schedule

8 paid hours a day, a 1-hour unpaid meal, two paid 15-minute breaks, and 5 working days a week.

Probation

Up to 6 months. Performance standards are shared at hiring, as the law requires, with checkpoints at 4 to 6 weeks, 3 months, and month 5.

Regular status

Team members who complete probation become regular employees with full security of tenure.

Exits & retirement

Resignation

Employees give 30 days’ notice. Highspring handles clearance, asset return, and final pay.

Termination for cause

Follows due process: a written notice of the issue, a chance to respond, and a written decision.

Business-driven exits

Redundancy or restructuring needs at least 30 days’ written notice to the employee and the Department of Labor and Employment, and separation pay based on the cause.

Retirement pay

Employees retiring at 60 to 65 with at least 5 years of service receive at least half a month’s pay for each year served.

Compliance & data

Data privacy

Personal data is protected under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, overseen by the National Privacy Commission.

Remote work

Remote roles follow the Telecommuting Act: agreed data-protection standards and the same rights as onsite employees.

Lawful contracting

Highspring is the employer and provides HR, supervision support, and performance management, structured to align with Department of Labor and Employment contracting rules.

Workplace conduct

Anti-harassment, anti-discrimination, and safety policies apply to every team member, remote or onsite.

FAQ

Common questions.

Who is the employer?+

Highspring is the employer in the Philippines. We handle employment contracts, payroll, statutory benefits, HR, performance management, and local compliance. Your team sets priorities, goals, and service expectations within the agreed model.

Will the team work only on our program?+

Yes. Resources are dedicated to your program, not pulled from a shared bench, with defined schedules, responsibilities, and escalation paths.

Who manages the team day to day?+

Highspring employs the team and provides HR, supervision support, and performance management. You set priorities, goals, and service expectations, and a U.S.-based delivery lead keeps both sides aligned.

How is our data protected?+

Team members work under the Philippine Data Privacy Act and agreed data-protection standards, with access set up to your security requirements. Remote roles also follow the Telecommuting Act.

Can people work onsite?+

Yes. Roles can be remote or onsite, and pricing reflects the option you choose.

How does pricing work?+

Each resource is priced by level and by remote or onsite setup, and billed monthly. Rates account for statutory burden, paid time off, and holidays. You receive final rates when candidates are presented.

How are holidays handled?+

Highspring publishes the official Philippine holiday calendar each year. Work on a regular holiday is paid at 200% of the daily rate, and work on a special non-working holiday at 130%, as Philippine law requires.

What if we need to end an assignment?+

Highspring manages the local employment process, required documentation, and statutory coordination. Any required notice, separation pay, or other termination-related costs are determined by the reason for separation and applicable Philippine law, and we walk through scenarios with you before you start.
